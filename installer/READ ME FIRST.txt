CleanListen
===========

Mutes bad language and cuts explicit scenes out of audiobooks you own.


INSTALL (one time)
1. Right-click the zip file, choose Properties, tick "Unblock" at the bottom, click OK.
   (Windows blocks downloaded programs until you do this.)
2. Right-click the zip file and choose "Extract All", then open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click "Install CleanListen".
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
     (It says that about any program that isn't from a big company.)
4. Wait 5-15 minutes while it downloads what it needs. It opens CleanListen when it's done.

After that, open CleanListen from the icon on your Desktop.


USING IT
1. Choose the audiobook files. Select every part if the book comes in pieces.
2. Optional: choose the ebook (EPUB, PDF or TXT) for the same book. It helps catch words
   the narrator mumbles.
3. Tick what to remove, then click Start cleaning.
4. If it finds explicit scenes, you'll see the text of each one. Untick any you want to keep.
5. The clean audiobook is saved in a "Clean" folder next to the original, along with a
   short report of everything that was removed.


FOR YOTO
Tick "Also split into chapter files" before you start. You get a "Chapters" folder with one
file per chapter, numbered in order. Upload those files to a Make Your Own card and you can
skip back and forth between chapters.
- No file is longer than an hour (Yoto's limit). Long chapters are split into "1 of 2", "2 of 2".
- Books over 5 hours are split into "Card 1", "Card 2" folders, one per card.
- If the narrator never says "Chapter One" etc., you get 30-minute "Sections" instead.


GOOD TO KNOW
- The audiobook has to be a plain file, like the MP3s you get from Libro.fm.
  Audible books are copy-protected and can't be used.
- Kindle ebooks are copy-protected too. The app still works without an ebook.
- Your original files are never changed.
- Closing the window mid-book is fine. Start it again with the same files and it
  picks up where it left off.
- Everything runs on your computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
